How fast can a project start?
Simple briefs start after scope and first payment are confirmed. Complex brand universes need a discovery call first.
Do you work outside Nigeria?
Yes. Worldwide from Lagos via secure links, cloud folders and OS-style project dashboards. Payment options discussed per project.
Are source files included?
Source files, usage rights and final formats are agreed before production starts — always part of the upfront scope conversation.
How do briefs and messages reach Hoopz?
Every form on this site — brief, contact, live chat request — delivers instantly to Hoopz via Telegram notification AND to hoopzblends@gmail.com. You can also reach Hoopz directly on Telegram or WhatsApp from the contact section.
What is the HBX Ecosystem OS?
HBX Ecosystem OS is a full AI operating system — 21 agents across 5 portals with voice, camera vision, browser, social connect, offline mode and SOVEREIGN self-update. Runs free on your PC using Groq AI. Email hoopzblends@gmail.com to get the package.
Can a project grow into a full system?
Yes. A logo or website can grow into a brand universe, content engine or full Cipher Sage OS module. The upgrade path is always available.
