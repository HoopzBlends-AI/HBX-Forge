## North Star

> *"Ground zero kobo → national infrastructure → world."*

Each phase delivers revenue, case studies, and credibility to fund the next.

The goal isn't to be the biggest company. The goal is to be the **infrastructure that lets emerging nations build their own digital future** — without debt, without colonial tech dependencies, without compromise on sovereignty.

That's a $100B+ market over the next 30 years. We're starting now.
